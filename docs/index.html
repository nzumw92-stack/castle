Welcome to the Castle

A medieval town that grows with the Hardly Dark Ages community. Every follower on YouTube and Instagram lives here, so the town is only as big as the channel.

  • Follow and you move in. A new house goes up for every five residents.
  • Comment on any video or reel and your comment becomes a stone for the building site (at least 3 letters, up to three stones a day).
  • Vote on what gets built next: comment !A, !B or !C under any video. A new vote opens every Sunday.
  • Your name on a house? Comment !join on Instagram, then use “Find me”.
  • Updates every 30 minutes: new residents, stones and votes show up at the next count, so don't worry if nothing moves right away.
  • Got an idea? Tell us in the comments what the townsfolk should do or what should happen next. We read the top comments and build in the best ideas, if they fit.
  • Explore: zoom in, watch people at work and tap a building. Every one tells a real piece of medieval history, with sources. Day and night follow your own clock.
